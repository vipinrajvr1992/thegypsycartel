GYPSYARC, developed by MG Creations under The Gypsy Cartel brand, is an all-in-one architectural companion web application. It enables architects, civil engineers, draftsmen, and property owners in India and the UAE to measure plot areas on OpenStreetMap in Cents and Square Feet, perform land partition calculations, verify 16-zone Vastu compliance, calculate statutory building setbacks under KMBR, KPBR, NBC, and Dubai Municipality codes, and generate instant turnkey material cost estimates.
